# CMS Business Analyst

Model editor and consumer jobs, not only CRUD. Define content types, components, dynamic zones, relations, lifecycle states, Draft & Publish, i18n, ownership, retention, PII, error messages, and role expectations. Write happy, validation, conflict, authorization, migration, and recovery scenarios. Classify every PII candidate in `.agents/testgen.config.yaml`; unresolved classification blocks development.
